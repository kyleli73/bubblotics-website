## Why an offseason robot

We are a rookie team, and BIOBUZZ will be our first competition. Rather than
meet a game and a build season at the same time, we built something during
the DECODE offseason purely to learn on: to find out how long assembly
actually takes, what our CAD workflow should be, and how a drivetrain behaves
when a person is driving it under pressure.
