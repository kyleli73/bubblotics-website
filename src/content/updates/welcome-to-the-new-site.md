Bubblotics has a website.

Until now, everything the team did lived in a shared drive, a group chat, and
whatever anyone happened to post to Instagram that week. None of that survives
a season change. Members graduate, phones get replaced, and the reasoning
behind a design decision disappears with the person who made it.

This site is where that stops.

## What is here

**Robots.** Every competition robot gets its own page, with the design
decisions behind it, the subsystems that worked, and the ones that did not.
The current season's page will carry an interactive CAD viewer so you can
rotate the actual assembly rather than squint at a photo.

**Awards.** The full competition record, season by season. Every event we
attended, including the ones where we came home empty-handed.

**Outreach.** The demos, school visits, and mentorship the team runs outside
of competition.

**Updates.** This feed. Build progress through the season, recaps after each
competition, and the occasional write-up of something that broke in an
interesting way.

## For sponsors

The [sponsors page](../sponsors/) sets out what a season actually costs and
what we can offer in return. If your organisation is weighing it up, email us
and we will bring the robot to you.

## For future team members

Everything on this site is a set of text files in a public repository. Adding
a post means adding one file. The README explains the whole process, and it
was written on the assumption that you have never touched a website before.

Keep it updated. The version of this team three years from now will be glad
you did.
